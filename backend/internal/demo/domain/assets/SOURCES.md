# Demo pictures

Used only by the demo seed (`backend/cmd/seed`); never shipped in the app.

They were cropped and re-encoded for the seed. The people in them are unrelated to the fictional demo names.
- **Crests** (`crest-*.png`): drawn for the demo (shield, club colours and letters); no third-party material.
